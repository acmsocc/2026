Venue

SoCC 2026 will be held at the Shangri-La Rasa Sentosa Resort & Spa, Singapore's only beachfront resort, located on Sentosa Island from November 18–20, 2026.

Conference Venue: Shangri-La Rasa Sentosa Resort & Spa, 101 Siloso Road, Sentosa, Singapore 098970

Shangri-La Rasa Sentosa Resort & Spa »

Getting here

From Changi Airport (SIN): The resort is approximately 30–40 minutes by taxi or private car from Singapore Changi Airport.

By public transport: Take the MRT to HarbourFront station (North East / Circle Line), then transfer to the Sentosa Express, bus, or a short taxi ride to the resort on Sentosa Island.

Accommodation: A discounted room block is available for attendees — see the Suggested Hotels page for booking details.
